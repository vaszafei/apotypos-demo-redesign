France Riots in the Greek Press
Apotypos · Greek press · France, 1–19 Oct 2026

How the Greek press framed the French riots

The 976 articles about the October 2026 unrest in France that 28 of the 36 outlets Apotypos monitors published from 1 to 19 October. It is the same analysis as the Facebook dashboard, applied to the press. Each headline is coded into one frame, and text measures count the words outlets use.

Read this before the numbers The denominator is 976 articles (9 off-topic ones are left out), collected for this study from outlet sitemaps, feeds and section pages. News247 blocks collection. Six outlets cover only 17–19 Oct, so the first two weeks are under-counted for them. An AI model (Claude) coded each headline into one frame, and nobody else has checked the labels. Headline frames are a standard unit in press studies, but a headline is not the whole article. The text measures are word counts over title and body, which are deterministic and reproducible. Publication times are dates only, so the order of copies within a day is unknown.

Coordination signals

Articles that look like mass-produced copy

Six behaviour signals, scored on every article: the same checks as the Facebook dashboard's individual-account section, adapted for newsrooms.

What a signal means A signal marks production patterns: wire copy re-run across outlets, no named author, templated shock headlines, an outlet re-running its own story. None of them shows paid or planted content. Agency copy and unsigned desk rewrites are normal newsroom practice. Treat three or more signals as a sign of churned copy, and a lead to check, not an accusation.

Limitations

What this corpus can and cannot show

  • The corpus is what could be collected on 19 October, not every article the outlets published. News247 is missing, and CNN Greece, Reader, Kathimerini, Ethnos, Insider and Iefimerida cover only 17–19 October.
  • One AI coder assigned the headline frames, with no second coder and no agreement score. Treat frame shares as rough, and outlet or group shares built on fewer than 50 articles as indicative only.
  • Word measures count presence anywhere in title or body, including quotes. They show the vocabulary an article carries, not the outlet's own voice. A rule-based own-voice test for migration was tried and dropped after a sample check failed.
  • Ownership groups come from the Apotypos media_groups table. 362 articles come from outlets with no listed group.
  • Coordination signals are rule-based. "No named author" counts newsroom and missing bylines together, and "very short" uses the extracted text, which can miss embedded video or live-blog content.
  • Times are dates only, so who copied whom inside a day cannot be told. Most copies share a wire source (ΑΠΕ-ΜΠΕ, AFP, Reuters) rather than copying each other.
