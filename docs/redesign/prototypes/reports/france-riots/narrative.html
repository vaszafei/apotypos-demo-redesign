"Immigrants, Islamists and the Palestinian flag": who carries the narrative

This page follows one narrative about the French riots: immigrants caused them, French immigration policy failed, the protests are a pro-Palestinian "intifada", and Israel is the West's front line against Islamism. It asks who produces the narrative in Israel, and where the same words occur in the Greek press and on Facebook.

At a glance

The narrative is made in Israel, borrowed from Trump and X, and rare in the Greek press

How often each part occurs

Percent of texts in which a word pattern for each part occurs (English, Greek, Hebrew, French). A pattern shows that a text uses the idea. It does not show that the writer holds the idea: most Greek mentions of immigration report Trump or Le Pen.

"Migrants / Islam blamed" is one category, because the rhetoric is the same. For the press, it counts texts that name immigrants, Muslims, Islam or Islamism. For Facebook, it is the coded cluster "Migration / Islam blamed" (each post was read).

Who produces it in Israel

46 Israeli articles touch Palestine, Islamism, Israel or immigration policy. Each one was read and given one voice.

Limitations

What this trace can and cannot show

  • The word patterns find mentions. One reader (Claude) read the 46 Israeli and 21 Greek candidates by hand to decide the voice. A second person did not check them.
  • The windows are different. The Greek corpus ends on 19 October, so Trump's "Muslim city" remark of 19 October is mostly outside it. Most Hebrew outlets are covered for 18–20 October only.
  • Facebook posts have no dates in this sample, so the trace cannot show whether a phrase was on Facebook before or after the press.
  • A shared phrase shows a common source or a copy. It does not show coordination or payment.
  • The Facebook sample comes from searches. Some searches name Jews, Mossad or synagogues, and these searches find most of the "intifada" posts.
