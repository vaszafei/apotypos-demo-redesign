"Immigrants, Islamists and the Palestinian flag": who carries the narrative

This page follows one narrative about the French riots: immigrants caused them, French immigration policy failed, the protests are a pro-Palestinian "intifada", and Israel is the West's front line against Islamism. It asks where the narrative started, who produces it in Israel, and where the same words occur in the French press, the Greek press and on Facebook.

At a glance

The narrative starts in the French right-wing media, Trump spreads it on 6 October, and Israeli right-wing outlets add the Israel angle

Limitations

What this trace can and cannot show

  • The article text that the crawler keeps can include sidebars and related links that the outlet added later. So the text counts can be too high, and a text match can look earlier than the article. The timeline uses headlines only for this reason.
  • French press: Libération refused all 297 requests and Le Parisien most of them, so they are almost absent. CNews pages give only 60–90 words of text, so CNews is under-counted. Live pages ("en direct") repeat the same lines many times.
  • Hebrew outlets for 1–17 October: Channel 14 (full, through its search API) and Walla, Ynet and N12 (only through section pages; N12 has no article before 18 October).
  • The word patterns find mentions. One reader (Claude) read the 46 Israeli and 21 Greek candidates by hand to decide the voice. A second person did not check them.
  • The windows are different. The Greek corpus ends on 19 October, so Trump's "Muslim city" remark of 19 October is mostly outside it.
  • Facebook posts have no dates in this sample, so the trace cannot show whether a phrase was on Facebook before or after the press.
  • A shared phrase shows a common source or a copy. It does not show coordination or payment.
  • The Facebook sample comes from searches. Some searches name Jews, Mossad or synagogues, and these searches find most of the "intifada" posts.
