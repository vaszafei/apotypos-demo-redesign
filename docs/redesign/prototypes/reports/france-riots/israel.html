The French riots in the Israeli media

This page shows how 18 Israeli outlets reported the October 2026 riots in France. It covers the large Hebrew outlets (Ynet, N12, Walla, Haaretz, Israel Hayom, Kan, Channel 14, Maariv, i24NEWS) and the English-language outlets (Times of Israel, Jerusalem Post, JNS). Each headline has one frame.

Limitations

What this corpus can and cannot show

  • The Google News sitemaps of the Hebrew outlets hold only the last two or three days (18–20 October). For 1–17 October: Haaretz and Israel Hayom through their full sitemaps, Channel 14 through its search API, and Walla and Ynet through their section pages (partly). Kan, N12, i24NEWS, Makor Rishon and Srugim have 18–20 October only.
  • Maariv's sitemap files stop in March 2026. Its 3 articles come from its world-news page only.
  • The 32 English-language articles come from an earlier crawl of 1–20 October.
  • One coder (a language model) gave each headline one frame. A second person did not check the codes.
  • On 22 October, Apotypos corrected one code. The Ynet headline of 19 October "תחזרו למקום שממנו באתם" quotes the footballer Emmanuel Petit. Its frame changed from soft news to migration blamed.
  • An article is in the corpus when its text names France two or more times and the unrest two or more times.
  • On 18 October, Haaretz wrote that the Israeli election is nine days away. Some outlets read the riots as a lesson for that election.
