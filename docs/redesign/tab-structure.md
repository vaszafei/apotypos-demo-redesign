## 2. The problems

- **Too long**: Μέσα › Επισκόπηση (3.4 screens), Μέσα › Θέματα (4.6), Ιστορίες › Επισκόπηση (4.0), Πρόσωπα › Επισκόπηση (2.1), Πρόσωπα › Φάκελος προσώπου (4.1), Πολιτική › Εκλογές (5.5, 15 cards).
- **Cards that answer another question**: the clickbait, byline and government-stance cards on Μέσα › Επισκόπηση (how outlets write, not how much); the per-outlet cards of the live page on Θέματα (chips, «Ένα θέμα, όλα τα μέσα», «Η ατζέντα κάθε μέσου») and on Ιστορίες (the stories × outlets matrix); the range chart and its measure switch on Πρόσωπα › Επισκόπηση (the switch only changes those two cards).
- **Two tabs, one kind of question**: Μετατοπίσεις measures topics, so it sits beside Θέματα as a sibling.
- **A section with one tab**: Πολιτική.
- **Detail pages**: none holds content of a tab. The outlet page is long (6.2 screens), but each card is about that one outlet.

## 4. One line of reasoning per change

1. **Μέσα › Πώς γράφουν (new)**: clickbait, bylines and the stance to the government describe how an outlet writes, not how much, and they made Επισκόπηση 3.4 screens long.
2. **Θέματα gets sub-tabs**: one tab held two pages, the topic table of the snapshot and the per-outlet cards of the live page (4.6 screens).
3. **Θέματα › Στον χρόνο** (merged into Θέματα › Επισκόπηση in #68): the time chart and the rising and falling topics answer "what changes", the table answers "what is largest". They are short enough to share one tab, so there is no separate sub-tab.
4. **Θέματα › Ένα θέμα**: the chips only pick the topic of «Ένα θέμα, όλα τα μέσα», so they stay together; `topics-v2.html?topic=N` opens this sub-tab.
5. **Θέματα › Ατζέντα μέσων**: the outlet × topic agenda is its own question and is 1100 px tall on its own.
6. **Μετατοπίσεις becomes a sub-tab of Θέματα**: it measures topics, and the move keeps Μέσα at 5 tabs with the new Πώς γράφουν.
7. **Ιστορίες › Ανά μέσο (new)**: the stories × outlets matrix of the live page asks which outlets wrote a story, not how stories spread across groups.
8. **Ιστορίες › Επισκόπηση, first page of the table**: the table shows 12 rows first (was 25) so that the charts that filter it stay on the same screen; «Δείξε 25 ακόμη» still adds 25.
9. **Πρόσωπα › Έμφαση και στάση (new)**: the measure switch changes only the strongest pairs and the range chart, so they are one question of their own.
10. **Φάκελος προσώπου gets sub-tabs Αντιφάσεις and Έλεγχος στοιχείων**: each of the two lists is 1700–2000 px long side by side, and they made the tab 4.1 screens; alone in its sub-tab each list takes the full width.
11. **Πολιτική gets 5 tabs**: 15 cards in one tab (5.5 screens) answer five questions: how much coverage, from which outlets, under which owners, on which topics, and by whom. (The first proposal had outlets and owners in one tab; at 2.06 screens it was too long, so it is two.)
12. **Routes**: every route of today opens the same file as before. A new tab is the same file with `?tab=`, so no page moves and no redirect is needed; `/topic` detail pages now go back to Θέματα, not to Μέσα › Επισκόπηση.

## 6. Limits

- «Πώς γράφουν για την κυβέρνηση» is 1894 px on its own (one row for each of the 36 outlets), so Μέσα › Πώς γράφουν is 2.5 screens whatever else it holds. Making the card shorter is restyling, which is #60.
- Μετατοπίσεις kept its cards (1768 → 1809 px); the sub-tab row of Θέματα adds the extra height.
- The question of each tab is not shown on the tab page (decision after review); it stays in the list of tabs in `pages.html`.
